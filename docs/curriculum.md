# Curriculum Overview

LinguaMCP uses a structured curriculum organized into **skill books**, **chapters**, and **lessons**.

## Structure

```
Skill Book (e.g., "Linking Words")
├── Chapter 1: Contrast
│   ├── Lesson 1: however
│   ├── Lesson 2: nevertheless
│   └── ...
├── Chapter 2: Addition
│   └── ...
└── Chapter 3: Cause & Effect
    └── ...
```

## Current Curriculum

### English: Linking Words

4 chapters covering the most important connecting words in academic and professional English:

| Chapter | Topic | Lessons |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Contrast | however, nevertheless, yet, nonetheless, on the other hand |
| 2 | Addition | furthermore, moreover, in addition, additionally, besides |
| 3 | Cause & Effect | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence |
| 4 | Condition | provided that, assuming that, unless, otherwise, supposing |

## Mastery System

- Each lesson is scored 1-5 during practice
- Score ≥ 4 = **mastered** (moves to next lesson)
- Score < 4 = **practiced** (will appear again)
- Daily sessions track which lessons were reviewed
